# Sharable Deck

**No Stranger Game** — a story-driven RPG you play in the browser. Your choices shape who you become. After the solo forest arc, you join other players in a shared village.

Use this page to explain the game to friends, collaborators, or anyone who has never heard of Nostr.

---

## The pitch (30 seconds)

You wake in a forest with no memory. Read the story, pick your responses, and slowly discover who you are — your **race**, your **class**, your stats, your reputation.

Early game is **yours alone**: binge through quests at your own pace. Once you reach the **Village**, the world opens up — chat, jobs, an arena, a market, elections, and community projects with other players.

No app store. No install. Log in with a Nostr account (or create one in seconds) and play.

---

## How it works

1. **Read & choose** — Story scenes appear on the **Play** tab. When it’s your turn, pick an option (or type your name when asked).
2. **Choices stick** — Decisions nudge your stats, personality traits, race leanings, and class leanings. There’s no “wrong” build — your path is the story.
3. **Explore the map** — Visit places like Silver Lake and the Waterfall. Some locations unlock new scenes.
4. **Track quests** — The **Quests** tab lists what’s available and what you’ve finished.
5. **Check your sheet** — The **Character** tab shows level, stats, race (once locked), class (once locked), skills, and gear.
6. **Re-read your story** — The **Chronicle** collects everything that happened to you in one scrollable log.

## Two phases of the game

### Phase 1 — The Forest (solo, bingeable)

- Location header reads **The Forest**.
- All eligible quests show up as soon as you qualify — no “wait until tomorrow” gates.
- You’re discovering the world, earning skills, and leaning toward a race and class through your choices.
- **Race lock** — At Silver Lake, your dominant leanings become your permanent subrace (e.g. Wood Elf, Atlantian, Orc). The lake “shows you who you are.”
- **Class lock** — When one of five archetypes (Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Healer, Ranger) reaches enough momentum from your choices, that path locks in.

You need both race and class locked before the Village opens.

### Phase 2 — The Village (shared world)

- Calendar pacing begins — the header shows **Day N** (real Eastern-time days from when you named your character).
- About **two new quests** can appear per in-game day.
- You gain **daily skill XP** and a **Day Report** summarizing what changed.
- The **Village** is a live hub with other players: chat rooms, jobs, arena fights, a tavern board, a market, mayor elections, and group building projects.

You can still travel back to the Forest from the Village.

## Who you can become

### Races — 13 subraces across 5 families

Your race is **not** chosen from a menu at the start. Quest choices quietly push you toward different peoples; Silver Lake makes it official.

| Family | Subraces |
|--------|----------|
| **Human** | Atlantians, Sunborn, River Kingdom |
| **Elf** | Night Elf, High Elf, Wood Elf |
| **Dwarf** | Dwarf, Gnome, Halfling |
| **Monster** | Orc, Troll, Goblin |
| **Animal** | Catfolk |

Each subrace comes with stat bonuses and flavor (traits, symbols, portrait on your character sheet).

### Classes — 5 archetypes

Your **class** is whichever archetype your choices pushed hardest before lock:

| Archetype | Fantasy |
|-----------|---------|
| **Warrior** | Front-line fighter, honor, steel |
| **Mage** | Arcane power, spirits, illusion |
| **Rogue** | Stealth, blades, precision strikes |
| **Healer** | Saving lives, support, cleansing |
| **Ranger** | Bow, wilderness, nature magic |

*(Finer specializations — Knight, Elementalist, Assassin, etc. — exist in the lore and will deepen over time; today the game tracks the five archetypes above.)*

### Skills

Three skills level up as you play: **Exploration**, **Foraging**, and **Melee Attack**. They unlock gates, flavor, and post-village jobs.

## Accounts & saving

- **Log in with Nostr** — A lightweight crypto identity (like a username that works across apps). Extensions like Alby or Primal work; the game can also generate a key for you.
- **Your save syncs** — Progress is stored on shared game relays and backed up in your browser. Play on another device with the same account and your story follows.
- **Reset anytime** — “Reset story” in the game menu starts fresh (your Nostr account stays; the character does not).

You do **not** need to understand Nostr to enjoy the game — think of it as “log in once, your save is portable.”

## Tone & feel

- **Literary, not grindy** — Short scenes, strong images, choices that matter narratively.
- **Not an MMO treadmill** — No endless fetch quests for XP; pacing respects real days after the Village.
- **Your story is yours** — The Chronicle is a personal novel you wrote through play.
- **Multiplayer adds spice** — Elections, market, arena, and chat — not required to enjoy the solo arc.

## Quick FAQ

**Is it free?**  
Yes — browser game, no purchase gate.

**Do I need crypto?**  
No wallet or Lightning needed to play the story. Nostr login is just identity.

**Can I play on my phone?**  
Yes — it’s a responsive web app.

**Will other players see my story choices?**  
They see your name, level, race, and class on activity feeds — not your private dialogue log.

**How long is the Forest arc?**  
Depends on how much you explore; it’s designed to be bingeable in a few sessions before Village pacing kicks in.
